# Support

Email **anton.duchenko2@gmail.com**. We answer within 2 business days.

Please include:
- your site address (`yourcompany.atlassian.net`);
- the work item key and the team, if it is about a notification;
- what you expected and what happened.

## Common questions
**A team member didn't get an email.** Check that they are a full member of the team (not an invited one), that they
can browse the work item, that the project is selected in **Jira settings → Apps → Team Notify** (or the list is
empty), and that they didn't make the change themselves.

**Nothing is sent at all.** Check that the app is switched on in its settings and that your subscription is active.

**"My teams' work" is empty.** It shows only open work items whose Team field is a team you are a member of.
